# Marquette Law Spring 2027 Class Planner

A single-page planner for Marquette University Law School's Spring 2027 term. It runs entirely in your browser, with no AI and no account, and works offline once loaded (you can also save the page and open the file directly).

- Every Spring 2027 law section, 1L sections, clinics, judicial internships, field placements and law journals
- Final exam dates, offering history ("usually spring only"), graduation requirement tags and RateMyProfessors ratings
- Week and month calendar, conflict and same-day finals warnings
- Unlimited plans, side-by-side comparison, a quick schedule builder and a schedule check
- Your plans are saved only in your own browser

Data was gathered on November 2, 2026 from Marquette's Class Search, course bulletin, the Law School's registration materials and RateMyProfessors. Seats, rooms and times change: confirm everything in CheckMarq before you register. Not affiliated with Marquette University.
