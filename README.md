# Law Class Planner (unofficial)

An unofficial, student-made planner for Marquette University Law School's Spring 2027 classes. **Not affiliated with, authorized, sponsored or endorsed by Marquette University or Marquette University Law School.**

It runs entirely in your browser, with no AI and no account, and works offline once loaded. Your plans are saved only in your own browser.

- Spring 2027 law sections, 1L sections, clinics, judicial internships, field placements and law journals
- Tentative final exam dates, offering history, graduation requirement tags and RateMyProfessors ratings
- Week and month calendar with conflict warnings, unlimited plans, side-by-side comparison, a quick schedule builder and a schedule check
